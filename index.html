    How the forecast works

    The page uses Holt-Winters exponential smoothing. It tracks three things in the monthly history: the current level of sales, the direction they are moving, and a typical multiplier for each calendar month. It then projects all three forward. The smoothing settings are chosen by trying a grid of values and keeping the ones that fit the history best.

    The likely range is a rough 95% band built from how far the model missed in the past, and it widens the further ahead it looks.

    The accuracy test hides the last 6 months, forecasts them, and compares with what really happened. The error shown is the average percentage miss.

    Limits: it needs at least 24 months of history to learn seasons (12 months gives a trend only). It does not know about promotions, stock-outs, price changes or one-off events. Treat the result as a baseline to adjust, not a promise.

    Your file is read in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere. Accepted columns: a date (any day or month is rolled up to months), a product or item name (optional), and units sold. Revenue is used if present, or worked out from a price column.
